Prague draught beer price, 2021–2024 Sourced

Average price of a 0.5 l draught beer in Prague restaurants, from till-system data.

2025–2026: I couldn't find a comparable till-system average after 2024. A separate crowd-sourced figure (Numbeo, Oct 2026) puts a domestic draught at about 69 Kč, but it's a different method, so it's not plotted. Pilsner Urquell announced a 2.6% price rise from 1 Oct 2026.

What drove it

1
Energy and operating costs, 2022–23
2
Draught beer VAT moved from 10% to 21% (Jan 2024)
3
Brewer price rises (2021, and 2.6% from Oct 2026)

Who drinks when Sourced

I found no Kč-per-age-group spending data. The nearest evidence is survey data on drinking patterns:

18
Ages 18–24: drink mostly on Fridays, little on other days
25
Ages 25–54: significantly more likely to drink Fri and Sat

Ages 55–64: tend to limit Friday drinking
Czech households also spend roughly 5,000 Kč per person a year on alcohol and tobacco combined (all channels), which puts the “Regular” scenario at about 10× that.
