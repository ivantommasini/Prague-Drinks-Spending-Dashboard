      What does a night out really cost?

      A half litre of beer costs 40 Kč on a Žižkov corner and 160 Kč on an Old Town Square terrace. This page prices the whole night, from the first pint to the club cover, and turns it into a yearly figure you can change.

          Year on year

          What pushed prices up

          • Oct 2021Plzeňský Prazdroj raised draught prices for the first time since 2018, by about 3% on average.
          • 2022–23Energy and operating costs led bars to add 5 to 10 Kč per half litre, more than the breweries themselves did.
          • Jan 2024VAT on draught beer moved from 10% to 21%. Prague restaurants added 3 to 4 Kč per pint, and 62% of surveyed pubs raised prices.
          • 1 Oct 2026Pilsner Urquell's maker announced a 2.6% rise on draught and bottled beer, its first since 2023.

          Sources and gaps

          Where the numbers come from

          “Sourced” figures are ranges quoted by the pages below, and they differ between guides. “Modelled” figures are my calculations from those unit prices. “Your data” comes from the tables you supplied.

          Not found

          • Monthly spending by age group. Only survey data on which days each age group drinks exists.
          • Spending by tourists compared with residents. The Tourist or local section compares prices instead.
          • A 2025 or 2026 average beer price from cash register data.
          • A per-person nightly spend figure for Prague. The Night types section is modelled.
          • Happy hour times confirmed by phone or in person. Several venues state their times on their own site, and some of those disagree with a listing. Most others rest on a single listing.
